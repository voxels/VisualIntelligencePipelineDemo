## Description
*(Estimated ~3,400 / 4,000 characters — paste as plain text into App Store Connect)*

Stop wondering. Start knowing.

Point your camera at anything — a product on a shelf, a document on a desk, a landmark, a person — and Visual Intelligence instantly reveals what you couldn't see before. It's the intelligent lens your iPhone was always meant to have.

——

**BUY SMARTER. EVERY TIME.**

Snap any product to get a full report before you spend a dollar — sustainability score, live price trends, active safety recalls, health impact, and a personalized "Buy Now or Wait" recommendation. An AR overlay anchors real-time scores to each product's position in space as you walk the aisle.

Our data comes entirely from free, open, public sources: Open Food Facts, CPSC safety recalls, FDA enforcement alerts, EPA compliance data, Energy Star certifications, and commodity price forecasts from the World Bank. No paid services. No data brokers.

——

**REMEMBER EVERYTHING.**

Every photo is automatically enriched the moment you take it. The exact venue. The music playing nearby. The current weather. Your library self-organizes into intelligent sessions — one tap shows you the full story of any day or place.

Subjects are lifted from backgrounds with clean edges. Documents are detected and perspective-corrected, making every word searchable. QR codes are followed and enriched automatically. Links from Safari, YouTube, or TikTok are saved directly into your library alongside your photos.

——

**ASK YOUR MEMORY ANYTHING.**

"What was that wine I photographed in Italy?" Just ask. The built-in AI chat searches across everything you've captured — photos, documents, products, and links — and cites exactly where to look.

——

**COMPLETELY PRIVATE.**

Everything runs on your device. All AI analysis — product scoring, smart tagging, face recognition, document reading — uses Apple's on-device frameworks. No accounts required. No photos uploaded. No data sold. Ever.

——

**FEATURES**

→ Commerce AR: Real-time product scores anchored in 3D space via ARKit
→ 7-engine ethical scoring: ESG, Health, Safety, Value, Durability, Social Proof, Total Cost
→ Two-phase pipeline: Items appear in under 2 seconds; deep AI runs silently in the background
→ Magical cutouts: Subjects lifted from any background, edges clean and sharp
→ Document scanner: Auto-detect, perspective-correct, and OCR — all in one tap
→ Smart sessions: Captures auto-grouped by place and time with AI summaries
→ Universal link hub: Save links from any app via the Share Sheet
→ Face recognition: On-device PhotoKit intelligence, nothing leaves your device
→ AI chat: Query your entire library in plain language with cited results
→ Music recognition: Apple Music and Spotify identification
→ Siri Shortcuts and App Intents integration
→ Home screen and Lock Screen widgets
→ CloudKit sync across all your Apple devices

——

*Requires iOS 26.0 or later. Apple Intelligence features require a compatible device. 3D scene generation requires a connected M-series Mac on the same Wi-Fi network.*

## App Review Notes (for App Review team)
*(Paste into the "Notes" field in App Store Connect — not shown to users)*

This app captures images and saves shared links using on-device Apple frameworks only (Vision, Foundation Models, MapKit, PhotoKit). No user images or personal data are transmitted to third-party servers. Product scoring uses public APIs (Open Food Facts, CPSC, FDA, EPA) queried at request time with 24-hour local caching. The app requires iOS 26 for Apple Intelligence features (SystemLanguageModel) and will gracefully degrade on earlier versions. The optional Mac edge node feature uses local network discovery (Bonjour) over TLS 1.3 — no internet traffic is involved.
