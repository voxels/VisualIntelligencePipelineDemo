## Description

**Visual Intelligence** is your AI-powered companion for understanding, organizing, and remembering the world around you. It transforms your camera into a tool that doesn't just see—it understands.

By combining advanced on-device AI with rich contextual data, Visual Intelligence layers meaning onto every capture, turning fleeting moments into actionable knowledge.

### Designed for Your Life

**For the Researcher**
Never lose a reference again. Snap a photo of a book cover, a document, or a whiteboard, and Visual Intelligence instantly captures the text, identifies the source, and organizes it into a dedicated session. Your visual notes are searchable, citeable, and ready for your next project.

**For the Shopper**
Make smarter decisions in an instant. See a product you love? Capture it to identify the item, find distinct variations, and save it to a collection. Whether you're comparing prices or building a wishlist, Visual Intelligence keeps your inspiration organized and accessible.

**For the Explorer**
Capture the feeling of a place, not just the view. When you snap a photo of a landmark or a hidden gem, Visual Intelligence enriches it with precise location data, current weather conditions, and historical context. Build a rich visual diary of your travels that remembers where you were, what the weather was like, and why it mattered.

### Powerful Features

*   **Intelligent Sifting:** Automatically detects and lifts subjects from their background, giving you clean, focused images for your collections.
*   **Rich Context:** Every capture is tagged with location, weather, and activity data, creating a complete memory of the moment.
*   **Multi-Modal Recognition:** Seamlessly scans QR codes, recognizes text, and identifies objects—all without switching modes.
*   **Smart Organization:** Your captures are automatically grouped into sessions based on time and intent, so you stay organized without the effort.
*   **Privacy First:** Built with on-device intelligence at its core. Your visual data is processed locally, ensuring your personal research and memories stay private.
