Pipeline Integration

Commerce enrichment runs as Stage ⑦ in LocalPipelineService.performCommerceEnrichment:

  1. Classify product (barcode or visual detection)
  2. Parallel fetch: ESG data + Government safety data (async let)
  3. Price trajectory + DFM nowcast (World Bank + BLS PPI)
  4. Score with all active strategies (7 engines)
  5. Learn preference weights from ownership history
  6. Generate ranked recommendations + SLM advisory
  7. Affiliate routing with RankingPolicy dimensions (ethical, price, speed)
  8. Record ScoreSnapshot for time-series charts
