# Privacy Policy for Visual Intelligence

**Effective Date:** February 12, 2026

**Visual Intelligence** is designed with a fundamental commitment to your privacy. We believe that your personal memories, research, and visual data belong to you and you alone.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.**

*   **No Accounts:** You do not need to create an account to use Visual Intelligence.
*   **No Analytics:** We do not track how you use the app.
*   **No Telemetry:** We do not collect crash reports or usage statistics.
*   **No Uploads:** Your photos and data are never uploaded to our servers.

## 2. On-Device Processing
All of the intelligence features in Visual Intelligence—including subject lifting (Sifting), object recognition, text recognition (OCR), and context enrichment—are performed **entirely on your device**.

*   **Apple Intelligence:** We utilize the Apple Neural Engine and on-device frameworks (CoreML, Vision) to process your data locally.
*   **Local Storage:** All your captures, sessions, and collections are stored securely in your device's local container or your personal iCloud container (if enabled by you for sync). We have no access to this data.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Visual Intelligence interacts with selected third-party services *only* to provide specific enrichment features at your explicit request.

*   **Apple Maps:** Used to provide location names and addresses. Location data is processed on-device and requests are anonymized by Apple.
*   **WeatherKit:** Used to fetch current weather conditions for your captures. Location data sent to Apple is anonymized.

We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel).

## 4. Your Data, Your Control
Since we do not collect your data, we cannot sell it, share it, or lose it. You maintain complete control over your information at all times.

*   **Delete Anytime:** You can delete any capture, session, or collection instantly from within the app.
*   **Export:** Your data is yours. You can export your images and text at any time.
